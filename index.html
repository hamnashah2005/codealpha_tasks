What is Phishing?

A deceptive attempt to steal your sensitive information.

Phishing is a cybercrime in which a target or targets are contacted by email, telephone or text message by someone posing as a legitimate institution.

Their goal? To lure individuals into providing sensitive data such as personally identifiable information, banking and credit card details, and passwords.

Social Engineering Tactics

🚨 Sense of Urgency

Attackers create a false sense of panic (e.g., "Your account will be suspended in 24 hours!") to rush you into making a mistake.

🔗 Deceptive Links

URLs that look almost correct but have slight misspellings (e.g., www.paypaI.com instead of paypal.com).

🎁 Too Good to be True

Offers of free iPhones, lottery winnings, or unexpected inheritance to prey on greed.

🎭 Authority Impersonation

Pretending to be your boss, the IT department, or a government agency (like the IRS) asking for sensitive data.

Can You Spot the Fake?

From: support@netflix-security-update.com
Subject: Action Required: Update Your Payment Method

Dear Customer,

We were unable to process your last payment. To avoid service interruption, please update your billing information immediately.

Update Payment Now
Hover to see red flags
  • Sender domain is suspicious (not @netflix.com)
  • Generic greeting ("Dear Customer")
  • Creates false urgency
From: service@intI-paypal.com
Subject: Unauthorized login attempt!

Hello,

We detected a login to your account from an unrecognized device in Russia. If this wasn't you, click the link below to secure your account.

Secure My Account
Hover to see red flags
  • Domain uses an uppercase 'i' instead of 'l' (intI vs intl)
  • Preys on fear and panic
  • Button links to a fake login portal
From: admin@yourcompany-it-support.com
Subject: MANDATORY: Office 365 Password Expiration

Attention Employee,

Your Office 365 password will expire in 2 hours. You must retain your current password by clicking the link below and authenticating.

Keep Current Password
Hover to see red flags
  • Impersonating internal IT (fake external domain)
  • Extreme urgency (2 hours)
  • "Keep current password" is not how real expiration works

Defense Best Practices

  • 🔍 Verify the Source: Always check the sender's actual email address, not just the display name.
  • 🖱️ Think Before You Click: Hover over links to preview the actual destination URL before clicking.
  • 🔒 Enable MFA: Use Multi-Factor Authentication whenever possible. Even if they get your password, they can't get in.
  • 📞 Contact Directly: If you get a suspicious email from a bank, don't click the link. Navigate to the bank's official website yourself.
